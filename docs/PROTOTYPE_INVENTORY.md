## Persistence and simulation audit

- `eg_proto_state_v1`: localStorage holds leads, inquiries, conversations/messages, tasks, notes, saved homes, activity and configuration. Replaced by the normalized database; no private CRM records should remain in browser storage.
- `eg_proto_session_v1`: localStorage claims a lead identity without verification. Replaced by Supabase Auth cookies for accounts and an independent random HttpOnly chat visitor cookie.
- `eg_proto_chat_v1`: per-tab sessionStorage holds conversation context/ID. Current localStorage stores only a non-secret conversation ID; transcript access also requires the hashed visitor cookie.
- `eg_admin_collapsed`: UI-only sidebar preference. Keeping a non-sensitive UI preference locally would be acceptable; it grants no access.
- Demo notifications, Google accounts, AI latency/replies, seeded metrics, reset/simulation and anonymous email matching must never be interpreted as working services or proof of account ownership.
